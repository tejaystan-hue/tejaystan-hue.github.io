ABO Academy

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

ABO Academy is an independently operated optical education and practice tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by ABO-NCLE or Walmart.

What this service does

ABO Academy provides practice questions, flashcards, optical math exercises, and a personal study dashboard. You can use parts of the tool without signing in, or sign in to associate study progress with your account.

Information collected

When you sign in with Google or email, the authentication service (Supabase) processes account identifiers such as your email address and account ID. ABO Academy stores your study preferences, optional display name, practice attempts, scores by subject, and missed-question history. The site uses browser storage for guest progress and temporary in-progress study sessions. Hosting and authentication providers may also process technical logs and connection information needed to operate and secure the service.

How information is used

Account information is used to authenticate you and associate your own progress with your account. Study information is used to show your results, practice history, and areas for review. The tool is not designed for instructor monitoring or employer reporting.

Service providers

Google may provide sign-in; Supabase provides authentication and account data storage; Render hosts the website. Their own privacy notices apply to information they process as service providers.

Sharing and visibility

The app is designed so each signed-in learner sees their own study data, not other learners' dashboards. The operator and service providers may have administrative or operational access needed to maintain the service. Do not submit patient records, confidential workplace data, or sensitive personal information.

Retention and your choices

Guest progress may remain in your browser until browser storage is cleared. Signed-in progress is retained in the service until it is deleted through an applicable account/data request or service maintenance process. Signing out does not itself delete account records. You may ask the operator to review or delete your study data and account; an in-app self-service deletion feature is not currently provided.

Security

Authentication and data hosting use third-party services. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Avoid sharing login links or credentials.

Changes

This notice may be updated as features or providers change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.

Privacy requests

A dedicated public privacy-contact address has not yet been published. The operator should provide a contact method before opening the service broadly.
